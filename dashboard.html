The three guardrails

Every model goes through the same three checks.

01

Locked test split

Candidates are compared and chosen on the development split only. The locked split is scored for the baseline and the chosen candidate. On the structure benchmark the split is scaffold-disjoint, so a near-copy of a training molecule cannot appear in the test set.

02

Label-permutation null

Shuffle the labels many times and rerun the whole pipeline. A real signal sits far to the right of that null. A leaking pipeline scores well even on shuffled labels, and the null shows it. On small benchmarks the null centers below 0.50, an effect of leave-one-group-out cross-validation that the README explains.

03

Baseline gate

A candidate passes only if its permutation p-value is below 0.05, its development AUC beats the null's 95th percentile, and its locked AUC beats the baseline. A random-score canary runs alongside as a check, and it has failed in every run so far.
